About

I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Huy Q. Nguyen.

I work on the mathematical analysis of partial differential equations arising from fluid dynamics, with a focus on stability and long-time behavior. If a steady state is perturbed, does the solution converge to a steady state, possibly a different one? In which topology? And when does convergence fail? So far I have studied these questions for Moffatt's magnetic relaxation equation and the incompressible porous medium (IPM) equation.

Before coming to Maryland, I received a B.Sc. in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics from Sharif University of Technology (2022).
